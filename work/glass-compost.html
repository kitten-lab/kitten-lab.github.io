Glass Compost

close to ship cut room

A chat mountain cannot be processed by skimming. Glass Compost is the live cut room: you take a log, you cut trunks and leaves by hand, you keep the grain. Compaction without throwing the strange away. The papers stay in the bench. This page is the machine.

why tree cut find cxr dress

why a cut room

Forestry archives the mountain. This bench is where you stand. A scene pass may propose a cut. You accept, edit, or reject. Nothing auto-accepts. The document is what you lock.

log

One chat bag. A mountain with a number. Lived order in the rail.

trunk

A stretch you cut. A scene, not a paste continuing. Title, shot, intensity.

branch

One turn. Stable chip. Parsed when you checked it.

leaf

A chunk inside a fat turn. Split on blank lines. Leftovers stay, marked low.

chips

Cite grammar lives here, not in the raw dump. Using a cite is how Pocket Go mints go.codes. Compost does not write Charlie. Cabinets there stay hand work.

the cut

Hands on the stream. A scene pass marks a shift. Accept makes a trunk. Reject leaves the wood. Cut done locks structure and prints the reading.
