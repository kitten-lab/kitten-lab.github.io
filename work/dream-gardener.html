Game

Dream Gardener

Design Cozy · the cave

Why

Not every question wants a terminal. Dream Gardener is a cozy cultivation system — grow, harvest, share — with a cave that keeps appearing. The softness is a way into the same research: meaning, return, what we plant when we cannot hold the whole map.

Status

Design documentation, public on GitHub. In the lineage of slow life games, aimed at a stranger kind of weather. Not shipped. Still mine.
