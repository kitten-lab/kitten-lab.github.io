Pocket Go

Active Worlds as pages
Screenshot next A little net. Address bar. Rooms you can walk.

Why

A mind cannot hold a whole inner internet at once. Chat logs, tabs, and folders leak. I needed a place where rooms could live as pages — a small WWW — so the worlds were not only something I remembered.

Pocket Go is that place: documents dressed as a net. You type an address. You enter a room. The thinking gets a body.

History

Before this it was My Pocket Internet, in a different environment. Same hunger, different coat. Pocket Go is the current public-facing shape of that idea: early-web chrome, hosts as folders, a start page that behaves like a home.

It is still growing. This page will pick up screenshots and a fuller lineage as I tell it.

Other pocket products, not this one: Pocket Notebook, Pocket Chapbook.
