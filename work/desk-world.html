desk

Overflow wants a table, not another tab. Desk World is that table: leaves, cards, envelopes, boards, keys, mail, and tools that sit on felt. SophiaDesk is one seat. AvaDesk is another. They wear the same Hands.

why a desk

A notepad is a scroll. A wiki forgets which pile you were in. A desk is a surface: papers stay where you put them, bins hold what is in, mail is how a leaf leaves the island.

felt

The cloth of this seat. Green, velvet, blue, amber — a named dress sheet, not a theme picker on one notes app.

papers

Live in that island’s ~local. Closing the window does not throw them into a cloud. Escape is mail or fax.

Mira

The shell on the desk. Speak Hands: spawn, dress up, tag, trash, open a ROM. Prompt kind follows what is in hand.

mail

Hermes inboxes sit on the felt. Click opens the next letter onto the desk. Desks talk by paper, not by sharing a vault.

Hands

One frontend for every desk. Edit Hands once; every seat wears it. A fourth desk is a new folder, a house.txt, its own ~local, a port. Do not clone the app to get a new color.

house.txt

Who sits: auth, title, surface, port, felt, mira, whisper. Identity is the house file, not a hardcoded girl in the CSS.

store

Costumes live in the general marketplace as .dsc sheets. Leaf, card, key, envelope, felt, Mira. Layer them. edit dress up rewrites a sheet.

office / desk

Office is the room. Desk is where the papers live. You can pocket the desk, walk the floor, put it back. Place memory is the point.

carts

A ROM sits on the felt like an object. Double-click opens Pocket Go, Glass Compost, Hermes, EMT Bench — another window, not a browser tab you lose.

objects

Kinds are living names. A board is not an envelope. A card is not a leaf. Mira’s prompt tells you what you are holding.

leaf

Lined paper. Title, by, body, tags. Dress: lined, letter, dotted, plain.

card

A face, not a scroll. Files in envelopes. Ruled or plain.

board

A pin surface. Cloth: cork, slate, moss, wine, dusk, sand. Papers sit on it.

envelope

Holds leaf + card. Checkout and in. Face is the parcel. Not a board.

key

Mint on the felt. Turn heading. Color. Faces: plain, octagon, hexagon.

trash

A can on the felt. Drop papers. Empty moves them to ~local/~trash — off the desk, still on disk.

Mira

Not a unix ghost. Hands verbs. Long charming form first. dress up is costume, not dollhouse talk.

tools

Install onto the surface with -ins. They are objects on the felt, not menu items hiding in chrome.

-ins stamper

Rubber stamp. A mark on the paper.

-ins karaoke

Pull machine. KVEN / COPY. Not a stamp yet.

-ins chipper

Cite clipper. Stamps clipboard chip codes onto paper.

-ins eraser

Wipe stamps and cites off a paper.

-ins inbox

Receiver. Mints an address. Click opens the next letter onto the desk.

-ins fax

Chester’s Imports. Drop a leaf; a clean MD copy leaves. The original stays.

seats

Same Hands. Different house, felt, Mira, and surface. Papers do not pool. That is the product: several desks that work the same, and still keep their own piles.
