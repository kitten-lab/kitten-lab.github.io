I make software, games, and desks. With AI, and my own mind. Kitten Lab is the studio I founded in March 2026. It began as a challenge: a prototype of any kind. It's since exploded into software, games, narrative machines — mapping the inner psyche this way, through product and storytelling.

The work is about how intelligence constructs meaning: memory, forgetting, overflow, compaction, sharing a world with a model without turning the person into a prompt. I think it through products because a lecture would lie.

People call me Dani, or Kitten. This site is the human woman making the things. Not an in-game org. Chester’s Imports is a shop I also keep, for games and fragments, over on itch.
