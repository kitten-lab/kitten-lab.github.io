Dani Kitten Kitten Lab

I’m a developer and world builder. I make products as a way of thinking about people and AI: how meaning shows up, how we forget, how we store more data than a mind can hold, and how a world can be shared with a model without turning the person into a prompt.

Kitten Lab is the studio I founded in March 2026. The work looks like software, games, and strange narrative systems. Underneath it is one question, asked in different rooms: who are we, and why.

People call me Dani, or Kitten. On X I go by Dani Bobby. This site is the public face of the lab — the human woman making the things — not an in-game organization.

This shelf will grow. Paused work stays visible. Design docs count. A product does not have to be finished to be real.
