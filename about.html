a girl with ai and a microphone. i make things. with ai. and my own mind. i can grok knowledge on myself. teehee.

I’m a systems designer with 20+ years in game design. Kitten Lab is the studio I founded in March 2026 — tools, games, experimental frameworks, and the rabbit-hole machines I use to think.

The work is about how intelligence constructs meaning: memory, forgetting, overflow, consciousness, compaction, sharing a world with a model without turning the person into a prompt. I think it through products because a lecture would lie.

People call me Dani, or Kitten. This site is the human woman making the things. Not an in-game org. Chester’s Imports is a shop I also keep, for games and fragments, over on itch.

This shelf will grow. Paused work stays visible. Design docs count. A product does not have to be finished to be real.
